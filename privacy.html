Privacy

Privacy Notes

This is a simple, plain-language explanation of how this portfolio website handles information. Travis Peakman built this site to be practical and transparent, not to collect more than is needed.

What this site currently does

In its first version, this website does not use analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, or a contact form. The only way to reach Travis directly through the site is the email link in the Contact section and footer, which opens your own email application.

What information may be collected

If you choose to email Travis using the link on this site, only the information you include in that email (such as your name, email address, and message) is shared, and it goes directly to Travis's inbox, not through this website or any third party.

Planned future features

This site is built to be ready for a contact form, basic analytics, and other tools later on. If any of those are added, this page will be updated first to explain what is collected, why, and which third-party services (if any) are involved, including any cookies or tracking technologies used.

Third-party services

This site does not currently use any third-party analytics, chatbot, or form-processing services. GitHub and LinkedIn links in the footer lead to those companies' own sites, which have their own separate privacy policies.
